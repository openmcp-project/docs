# Personas

| | Persona | Description | Common Tasks | Starting Point |
| :-: | :------ | :---------- | :----------- | :------------- |
| <Settings size={28} color="var(--teal-6)" /> | **Platform Owner** | Hosts and operates the platform for their organization. Selects and configures the [Cluster](../../developers/clusterprovider/design), [Platform](../../developers/platformservice/design), and [Service Providers](../../developers/serviceprovider/design) to match their company's needs. | <ul><li>Set up the platform</li><li>Enable services on the platform</li><li>Ensure health of the platform</li></ul> | [Operators Guide →](/operators/overview) |
| <User size={28} color="var(--teal-6)" /> | **End User** | Orders `ControlPlanes` from the platform. Picks from the available services to choose the best tools for orchestrating their cloud landscape. | <ul><li>Order `ControlPlanes` on the platform</li><li>Build cloud landscapes using `ControlPlanes`</li><li>Ensure health of cloud landscapes</li></ul> | [End User Guide →](/users/getting-started) |
| <Puzzle size={28} color="var(--teal-6)" /> | **(Platform) Service Provider** | Teams providing a service _(e.g. OCM, Flux, any APIs)_ to end users on the platform. | <ul><li>Offer services on the platform</li><li>Contribute to service providers</li><li>Ensure health of services offered</li></ul> | [Developer Docs →](/developers/overview) |
| <Wrench size={28} color="var(--teal-6)" /> | **Contributor** | Extends, fixes, or adjusts Cluster, Platform, or Service Providers. | <ul><li>Enrich service features, security and stability</li></ul> | [Community →](/community/overview) |
