# Personas

| | Persona | Description | Responsibility | Common Tasks | Starting Point |
| :-: | :------ | :---------- | :------------- | :----------- | :------------- |
| <Settings size={28} color="var(--teal-6)" /> | **Platform Owner** | Operates the platform within their organization. Selects and configures the [Cluster](../../developers/clusterprovider/design), [Platform](../../developers/platformservice/design), and [Service Providers](../../developers/serviceprovider/design) to match their company's needs. | Hosts the platform and ensures its healthy operation. | Set up the platform · Enable services on the platform · Ensure health of the platform | [Operators Guide →](/operators/overview) |
| <User size={28} color="var(--teal-6)" /> | **End User** | Orders `ControlPlanes` from the platform. Picks from the available services to choose the best tools for orchestrating their cloud landscape. | Relies on a healthy platform and observes the health of their cloud landscape. | Order `ControlPlanes` on the platform · Build cloud landscapes using `ControlPlanes` · Ensure health of cloud landscapes | [End User Guide →](/users/getting-started) |
| <Puzzle size={28} color="var(--teal-6)" /> | **(Platform) Service Provider** | Teams providing a service _(e.g. OCM, Flux, any APIs)_ to end users on the platform. | Observes the health of the services they offer. | Offer services on the platform · Contribute to service providers · Ensure health of services offered | [Developer Docs →](/developers/overview) |
| <Wrench size={28} color="var(--teal-6)" /> | **Contributor** | Extends, fixes, or adjusts Cluster, Platform, or Service Providers. | Keeps the platform ecosystem healthy and up to date. | | [Community →](/community/overview) |
